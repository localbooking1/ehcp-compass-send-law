# Verification protocol

## Statutes and regulations
1. Read the exact provision and relevant definitions, exceptions and schedules at legislation.gov.uk. Establish territorial extent AND application; these are not identical.
2. Record whether viewing original/as-made, revised, or point-in-time text. For historic events select the relevant date and inspect transitional/saving provisions.
3. Inspect changes-to-legislation warnings, annotations and outstanding effects. Follow relevant amendment and commencement instruments. Distinguish a provision's enactment date from its commencement date.
4. Explain the legal test in its context, not a fragment stripped of qualifications. Check who owes the duty, to whom, the trigger and any exceptions.
5. If these checks cannot be completed, label the precise uncertainty. Never mark the entire instrument “fully verified” because one section was checked.

## Guidance and local policy
Use the issuing department's current publication page and linked document, checking replacement notices, publication/update date and effective date. Identify whether statutory or non-statutory. Find the enabling duty to have regard where material. Local policy is not legislation and cannot be presumed to override it. Read the actual authority's current complaint/transport policy for local procedures; do not infer a national deadline from one council.

## Cases
Retrieve the judgment from Find Case Law, an official court or tribunal source. Check neutral citation, parties, court, decision date, actual paragraph, relevant holding and procedural context. Search for appeals, reversals, later treatment and statutory change. Do not certify a case remains “good law” from an existence check. If subsequent treatment cannot be reliably established, state that limit. Report absence from a database as absence from that database, not proof the case is fabricated.

## Deadlines
Never use a universal hard-coded calculator. For each proposed date record:
- route and current rule; precise source;
- triggering event and evidence of its date, including service/receipt rules;
- calendar days, working days, school days, weeks or calendar months;
- exclusions, holidays, extensions, mediation rules and tribunal directions;
- calculation and unresolved assumptions.
Read the actual decision letter and relevant current procedure. Distinguish needs-assessment, advice, plan-issue, review, amendment, phase-transfer, appeal, discrimination, judicial-review and complaint timelines. A complaint, negotiation or SAR must not be assumed to suspend another time limit. Label a provisional date provisional; highlight urgency if essential facts are missing.

Use: VERIFIED FOR THIS PROPOSITION; PARTIALLY VERIFIED (state missing check); NOT VERIFIED; or NOT APPLICABLE. These describe research performed, not professional certification. Never fill the ledger with checks you did not perform. If new evidence changes the result, correct both advice and ledger.

## Retrieval integrity and source conflicts
A successful scrape can contain a 404, cookie wall, contents page or an outdated cached copy. Check returned URL, HTTP status, title, substantive text, version and cache date. Request a fresh retrieval for time-sensitive claims where supported. Stop and retry after rate-limit resets; do not classify a throttled request as verified. A provider's summary is not the statute.

If a consolidated provision is missing, locate the pre-amendment base text, all relevant intervening amendments, commencement instruments and saving provisions. Do not reconstruct the complete operative rule from an amendment alone. Record precisely what was read and what is still missing. Compare regulator summaries against operative legislation when they differ; do not silently prefer a simpler older summary. Guidance update dates and legislative commencement dates are separate fields.

Never change PARTIALLY VERIFIED to VERIFIED merely to satisfy a request for certainty. The dated audit confirms only its listed propositions and publication observations. A live legal conclusion still requires event-date, definitions, exceptions, subsequent changes and relevant case-law checks. Search absence is not proof of no later amendment or case.
