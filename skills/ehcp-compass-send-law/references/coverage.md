# A–Z research coverage

This map routes research; it does not declare every legal rule below verified or promise exhaustive coverage. Consult the official source registry and verify exact provisions before applying any rule.

| Area | Research questions and starting authorities |
| --- | --- |
| A — Assessments and advice | Requester, assessment versus plan threshold, evidence, professional advice, refusals, reassessment; Children and Families Act 2014 Part 3 and SEND Regulations. |
| B — Best endeavours and SEN support | School/college responsibilities, graduated approach, SEN information, SENCO, participation; CFA Part 3 and SEND Code. |
| C — Contents and consultation | Plan sections A–K, needs/provision/outcomes, specificity, quantified support, placement consultation; CFA and SEND Regulations/Code. |
| D — Disability discrimination | Equality Act definitions, reasonable adjustments, exclusions, responsible body, forum and remedies; Equality Act and current HMCTS guidance. |
| E — Education outside school | Distinguish EOTAS, elective home education, alternative provision and health-related absence; CFA, Education Act 1996 and current DfE guidance. |
| F — Funding and personal budgets | Responsible bodies, personal budget/direct payment conditions and consent, local policies; CFA and current direct-payment regulations. |
| G — Governance and accountability | Identify LA, school/trust, health commissioner and provider duties separately; statutory versus local complaints, maladministration versus unlawful decision. |
| H — Health | Health needs/provision, medical support at school, therapy classification, continuing care interface; CFA, NHS framework and current guidance. |
| I — Inclusion and placement | Mainstream rules, parental/young person preference, named placement, independent institutions, admissions and statutory exceptions; CFA and school admissions framework. |
| J — Judicial review | Reviewability, alternative remedies, urgency, pre-action steps and limitation; CPR Part 54, current protocol and specialist advice. |
| K — Keeping records | Chronology, evidence provenance, parental/young person access, education records versus SAR; UK data law, ICO and education-record regulations. |
| L — Local offer and participation | Information/advice, local offer, consultation, child/young person views and support to participate; CFA and SEND Code. |
| M — Mediation | Adviser contact, certificate, exceptions, effect on appeal deadline; current CFA provisions, regulations and HMCTS guidance. |
| N — Non-delivery and delay | What is specified, who must secure it, actual missed provision, excuses and remedy route; CFA, relevant judgments and Ombudsman jurisdiction. |
| O — Ombudsmen and complaints | LA, children's social care statutory procedure, school/trust, health and data routes; check exclusions where a tribunal remedy exists. Do not promise compensation. |
| P — Preparing for adulthood | Post-16 rights, capacity, age 19–25, education/training objectives, adult-care transition, employment/higher-education boundaries; CFA, Care Act, MCA and guidance. |
| Q — Quality of evidence | Match each identified need to provision and outcome; expert evidence, disputed facts, working documents; no diagnosis invented from behaviour. |
| R — Reviews and changes | Annual review, amendment, reassessment, phase transfer, relocation, cessation and appeal rights; CFA and SEND Regulations. |
| S — Social care and safeguarding | Children's needs, short breaks, carers, adult-care transition, safeguarding interface; Children Act, CSDPA, Care Act and current safeguarding guidance. |
| T — Transport | Compulsory-school-age, sixth-form-age and adult transport regimes, individual suitability and review routes; Education Act and current DfE/local guidance. |
| U — Upper Tribunal | Permission, error of law, review/set-aside versus appeal, current procedural deadlines; HMCTS SEND20 and chamber rules. |
| V — Voice and representation | Child/young person's wishes, parental responsibility, competence/capacity, supporter authority; CFA, MCA and current procedural rules. |
| W — Attendance and exclusions | Health-related inability, part-time timetables, informal exclusion, suspension/permanent exclusion, attendance enforcement; current DfE guidance, education law and equality duties. |
| X — Cross-boundary issues | Moving authority, looked-after children, youth detention, hospital provision and out-of-area placements; identify specific statutory regime before advice. |
| Y — Young people and carers | Decision-making rights, adult services, carers' assessments, transition and ceasing plans; avoid treating age alone as dispositive. |
| Z — Zero unsupported citations | Audit every authority, deadline and asserted breach; distinguish reform proposals from law; use the verification ledger. |

## Adjacent issues
School admissions, bullying, restraint/restrictive practice, accessibility planning, exams/access arrangements, early years, looked-after children, care leavers, housing adaptations, welfare benefits and legal aid may intersect. Research the specific applicable framework and regulator; signpost specialist areas without presenting this skill as comprehensive expertise in them. For Scotland, Wales, Northern Ireland or cross-border matters, stop applying England assumptions and obtain jurisdiction-specific authority.
